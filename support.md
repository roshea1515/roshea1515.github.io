# Fore! Support

Something not working, or an idea for the game? Email [oshea1515@gmail.com](mailto:oshea1515@gmail.com) and include
your iPhone model and iOS version.

## Common questions

**Purchases didn't unlock.** Open Courses and tap **Restore Purchases**, signed in with the Apple Account you bought
them on.

**Remove Ads came with a free course but I can't see my pick.** On a new iPhone without iCloud, the shop asks you to
pick again.

**The game isn't on my TV.** Connect with AirPlay screen mirroring from Control Center; Fore! then shows the course on
the TV and turns your phone into the club.

**Ad privacy choices (UK and Europe).** Settings in the game has **Ad privacy choices** to change your answer at any
time.

See also the [Privacy Policy](../privacy/).
